You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

Send an email from the email address associated with your Step Up account to james_dyble@live.com with the subject “Step Up account deletion”.

Email a deletion request

We may ask you to verify that you are the account holder. After verification, we will delete or anonymise the personal data associated with your account, except for limited information that we may need to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention or service-integrity reasons.

What will be deleted

This may include your account profile and Step Up content associated with your account, such as goals, plans, reflections, decision-drill responses, impact-log entries and other user-created content.
