1. About Step Up

Step Up is a career development and coaching app designed to help users set goals, build development plans, practise decision-making, record evidence of impact, and track progress over time.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Step Up collects, why we use it, how it is handled, and the choices available to you.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create or use a Step Up account, we may process information such as your email address, account identifier, authentication status, and related account details. Authentication is handled using Supabase.

Information you provide in the app

Step Up may store information that you choose to enter, including:

  • career goals and development objectives;
  • job descriptions or role-related information you paste into the app;
  • daily intentions, reflections, plans and actions;
  • decision-drill responses;
  • impact-log entries and evidence of progress;
  • feedback or support messages you submit; and
  • other text you choose to provide while using Step Up.

Technical information

The app and the services that support it may process limited technical information such as app version, device or operating-system information, timestamps, diagnostic information, and service logs where needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the app.

3. How we use your information

  • create and manage your account;
  • provide, save and synchronise your Step Up experience;
  • generate coaching, development guidance and other app content;
  • remember your goals, actions, plans and impact history;
  • respond to feedback and support requests;
  • maintain security and prevent misuse; and
  • diagnose problems and improve reliability and performance.

4. AI processing

Some Step Up features use artificial-intelligence services to generate coaching content, summaries, prompts, exercises or recommendations. When you use those features, relevant text that you submit may be sent to an AI service provider so that the requested response can be generated.

We aim to send only the information needed to provide the relevant feature. You should avoid entering information that you do not want processed as part of an AI request.

5. Service providers

We use third-party service providers to operate Step Up. These providers process information on our behalf for purposes such as authentication, database hosting, infrastructure and AI-powered functionality.

  • Supabase — account authentication, database and backend services.
  • AI service providers — processing relevant user inputs to generate AI-powered coaching content.

These providers may process data in countries other than the country where you live and are subject to their own contractual and legal data-protection obligations.

6. Data retention

We retain account information and user-created content for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide Step Up. We may retain limited information for longer where reasonably necessary for security, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or service integrity.

If you request deletion of your account, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise the personal information associated with your account, subject to any information we are required or permitted to retain.

7. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your Step Up account and associated personal data at any time.

Email james_dyble@live.com with the subject “Step Up account deletion”, or use our account deletion page.

We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder before completing deletion.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Step Up is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly design the service for children.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights in relation to your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, and to object to certain processing.

To exercise a privacy right, contact us using the email address below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Step Up develops or as legal and service requirements change. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.
